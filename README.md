# AI Governance & Risk Register

## 🏛 Project Overview

The AI Governance & Risk Register is a Streamlit-based governance platform designed to support responsible Artificial Intelligence (AI) adoption within an organisational environment.

This project was inspired by a Council-wide AI Register initiative aimed at understanding how AI tools and AI-enabled systems are being used across the organisation. As AI capabilities become increasingly embedded in productivity tools, business systems, and third-party platforms, organisations require greater visibility over AI usage, cyber security risks, privacy impacts, and governance responsibilities.

The platform provides a central register for capturing AI tools, their business purpose, vendor information, department usage, data classification, and risk level.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Identify where AI tools and AI-enabled systems are currently being used.
- Understand how staff may use AI technologies in day-to-day work.
- Capture emerging AI opportunities, risks, and support requirements.
- Strengthen cyber security oversight of AI-enabled systems.
- Improve protection of organisational information and data.
- Support privacy, governance, audit, and compliance requirements.
- Establish a central AI Register to support ongoing monitoring and risk management.

---

## 🤖 What is an AI Register?

An AI Register is a central record of AI use across an organisation. It helps document:

- What AI tools or features are in use.
- What the tools are used for.
- Which departments or teams use them.
- Which vendors or systems they come from.
- What type of data is processed.
- How cyber security, privacy, and governance risks are managed.

This includes both standalone AI tools and AI features embedded within third-party systems.

---

## 🔒 AI Risk Areas Covered

### Cyber Security Risks

- Data leakage through external AI platforms.
- Unauthorised access to sensitive information.
- Prompt injection or malicious AI input attacks.
- Third-party vendor security weaknesses.

### Privacy Risks

- Exposure of personal or confidential information.
- Inappropriate use of sensitive data.
- Lack of visibility over where data is processed or stored.

### Governance Risks

- Lack of transparency around AI usage.
- Unclear accountability for AI-generated outputs.
- Limited monitoring of AI tools used across the organisation.

### Compliance Risks

- Failure to meet audit and reporting requirements.
- Inconsistent documentation of AI systems.
- Potential misalignment with internal policies and digital risk frameworks.

### Ethical Risks

- Bias or unfair outcomes.
- Lack of explainability.
- Over-reliance on AI-generated outputs without human review.

## 🔄 AI Governance Lifecycle

The AI Governance & Risk Register follows a structured governance workflow:

1. **AI Survey & Discovery**

   * Identify AI tools and AI-enabled systems currently used across the organisation.
   * Capture information about business purpose, users, and vendors.

2. **AI Registration**

   * Register AI tools within the central AI Register.
   * Maintain an inventory of approved and emerging AI technologies.

3. **Risk Assessment**

   * Assess data sensitivity and governance impacts.
   * Evaluate cyber security, privacy, compliance, and operational risks.

4. **Risk Classification**

   * Automatically assign a risk level based on predefined governance criteria.
   * Categorise tools as Low, Medium, or High Risk.

5. **Governance Dashboard**

   * Visualise AI adoption across departments.
   * Monitor organisational AI risk exposure.

6. **Reporting & Audit Support**

   * Export AI Register records.
   * Support governance reviews, compliance reporting, and audit activities.
